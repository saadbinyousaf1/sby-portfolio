Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit saadbinyousaf.dev and how it is used.

Information I collect

This site does not ask you to create an account. If you email me, I receive your email address and anything you include in your message, and I use it only to reply.

Free tools

The free HighLevel tools on this site run entirely in your web browser. Text you type, contact lists you load into the CSV cleaner, and details you enter into generators are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to any server I control.

Some tools remember your last inputs, saved UTM links, or checklist progress using your browser's local storage, so they are still there when you return. This data stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Tool output, such as A2P 10DLC wording or review request templates, is general guidance and not legal advice.

Affiliate links

If you click one, HighLevel's website may set its own cookies to record that the referral came from this site, so a commission can be credited if you sign up. That happens on HighLevel's site, under HighLevel's privacy policy, and only after you choose to click. This site never sets a referral cookie in the background or redirects you automatically. I don't receive your name, payment details or account information from HighLevel. See the affiliate disclosure for details.

Third-party services

Fonts and animation libraries are served from this site itself, so no third-party font or script provider receives your IP address when you load a page.

Your choices
