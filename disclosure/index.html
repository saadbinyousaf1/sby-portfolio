Last updated 13 November 2026

Which links are paid links

Links and buttons that take you to gohighlevel.com to start a trial, view plans, see the Founder plan, join the Bootcamp or upgrade an account are paid links. In the guides, each one sits next to a line saying I earn a commission, and each guide carries a disclosure at the top. The "Get started" panel on guide pages and the HighLevel links in the site footer are paid links too.

Links to HighLevel's help centre in the Sources section of each guide are ordinary links. I earn nothing from those.

  • I make no promises about results. What you get from HighLevel depends on your business and the work you put in.

Client work

I also build HighLevel systems for agencies and businesses as paid client work. That is separate from the affiliate programme. If you hire me, you can sign up for HighLevel through any link you like, or none.
