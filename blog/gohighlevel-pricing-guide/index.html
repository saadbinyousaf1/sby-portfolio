The prices at a glance

HighLevel sells three plans with published prices, plus a custom Enterprise tier. These figures were checked on 14 November 2026.

Prices in US dollars before tax. Usage charges are extra on every plan. HighLevel renamed its $497 Agency Pro plan to Founder, and some of its own pages still use the old name.

The headline numbers are simple. The parts that catch people out are the usage charges and choosing a tier they don't need yet, so most of this guide is about those two things.

What every plan includes

The pricing page lists the same set of core features for all tiers. That means the $97 plan is not a cut-down product. On any plan you get:

  • Unlimited contacts and unlimited users
  • The CRM, pipelines and a unified conversations inbox
  • The workflow builder for automation
  • Booking calendars
  • The website, funnel and form builders
  • Email and SMS marketing, and inbound and outbound calling, both billed by usage
  • Reputation management, social media planning, payments and invoicing
  • Courses and communities
  • The mobile app
  • AI tools including Conversation AI, Voice AI, Content AI and Reviews AI, mostly billed by usage

The tiers differ on three questions: how many separate businesses you can run, whether you can pass usage costs on to clients, and whether you can sell the platform as your own product.

Starter: $97 a month

Starter includes every core feature and three sub-accounts. A sub-account is a self-contained workspace for one business, with its own contacts, phone numbers and automations.

Choose it if you run one business, or you are a freelancer or new agency with one to three clients. For a single business there is no functional reason to pay more. The higher tiers add agency capabilities, and none of them make the CRM or the automations work better.

You will outgrow it when you need a fourth sub-account, or when you want clients to pay their own messaging costs through rebilling.

Unlimited: $297 a month

Unlimited includes everything in Starter and adds:

  • Unlimited sub-accounts. The fortieth client costs the same platform fee as the fourth.
  • A white-label desktop app. Clients log in to your brand and your domain.
  • Rebilling at cost. Each client's phone and email usage can be charged to that client's card, without a markup.
  • Basic API access and user and agent reporting.

Choose it if you manage four or more client accounts, or you expect to within a couple of months. At four clients the plan works out to about $74 per client, and the figure falls with each client you add.

Rebilling matters more than it sounds. Without it, every text and call your clients' accounts make is charged to your agency wallet, and you have to recover the money through your own invoices. With it, usage follows the client.

Founder: $497 a month

Founder includes everything in Unlimited. Its defining additions are commercial:

  • SaaS Mode. You create your own plans and prices, and HighLevel automatically creates a sub-account and bills the customer when they sign up.
  • Rebilling with a markup. You can charge clients more than cost for texts, calls, email and AI.
  • Advanced API access.
  • AI Employee Starter, a $20-a-month per-location AI plan that only Founder accounts can offer.
  • Additional features that HighLevel lists as Founder-exclusive, including industry-specific tools, project management, ticketing, events and meeting rooms.

The Founder page also lists community perks and partner programmes. Several were marked "coming soon" or given estimated dates when I checked, so judge the plan on what is live. You can read the current list on HighLevel's Founder plan page(I earn a commission).

Choose it if you have a concrete plan to sell software subscriptions under your own brand, or to mark up usage across enough clients to cover the extra $200 a month. Wanting to do so one day isn't enough reason. You can move up from Unlimited the week you actually need SaaS Mode.

Reselling is a business, with obligationsIf you sell HighLevel as your own product, your customers come to you for support, billing questions and cancellations. Nobody can promise what you will earn from it. Budget time for support before you count on the revenue.

Monthly or annual?

Annual billing costs ten times the monthly price, so you get twelve months for the price of ten.

Plan12 months paid monthlyAnnual priceDifference
Starter$1,164$970$194
Unlimited$3,564$2,970$594
Founder$5,964$4,970$994

My recommendation is to start monthly. The first two or three months are when you find out whether your team actually uses the platform and whether you picked the right tier. A year paid in advance on the wrong plan costs more than the discount saves. I could not find refund terms stated on the annual page, so read HighLevel's terms of service before you commit.

Annual billing makes sense once HighLevel is clearly part of how you operate and your tier has been stable for a few months.

Sure you'll stay a year?

If you've already used HighLevel long enough to know it fits, annual billing on Starter or Unlimited costs the same as ten months paid monthly.

Usage charges: what's billed on top

Your plan pays for the software. Anything that costs HighLevel money each time you use it is charged separately, from a prepaid wallet that tops itself up. The rates below are for the United States and Canada, taken from HighLevel's help centre.

What you usePublished rate

Toll-free number$2.15 a month
SMS, sent or received$0.00747 per segment, plus a carrier fee of about $0.003 to $0.005 on each outbound US message

A "segment" is up to 160 plain characters. Longer messages and messages with emoji use more segments. Calls are billed in whole minutes. Rates change, so check the linked sources before budgeting.

AI pricing

AI features can be paid per use with no monthly fee, or through a per-sub-account plan. HighLevel's pricing guide lists AI Employee Growth at $50 a month and AI Employee Unlimited at $97 a month, each per enabled sub-account, with the Unlimited plan covering Conversation AI, Voice AI, Reviews AI and Content AI under a fair-use policy. Voice AI calls still incur phone charges. Because these fees are per sub-account, an agency enabling AI for ten clients pays ten times. The AI pricing guide has the full breakdown.

The free trial: 14 days or 30

HighLevel's standard trial is 14 days on any plan. Three details from the pricing page are worth knowing before you start:

  • Billing begins automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel first.
  • Usage fees for texts, calls and other third-party services can apply during the trial.
  • Plans are month-to-month, and you can cancel from the dashboard.

There is also a 30-day trial attached to HighLevel's live Bootcamp, a free one-hour Zoom session where its coaches guide new users through first setup. It is aimed at people starting out, asks for payment details at signup, and is designed to be followed on a laptop or desktop. The session isn't recorded. If you are new and would use the hour, the longer trial is the better deal: HighLevel's Bootcamp page(I earn a commission) has the current schedule.

Whichever trial you take, put the end date in your calendar on day one. The free trial guide has a day-by-day plan for using it.

Which plan fits you

A single business

Starter. You need one sub-account and you get three. Spend the money you save on usage and on getting the setup right.

A freelancer with two clients

Starter, until the day you sign a fourth account. You will carry clients' usage costs yourself, so build them into your fee.

An agency with six clients

Unlimited. You are past the three-account limit, and rebilling at cost stops usage from eating your margin.

An agency planning to sell software subscriptions

Founder, once you have a priced offer and at least a few customers ready to buy. Until then, Unlimited does everything you need to deliver client work.

Not sure yet

Starter, paid monthly. HighLevel lets you upgrade from the dashboard whenever you like, and moving up is easier than discovering you overbought.

Already on HighLevel? When to upgrade

If you already have an account, the question is whether an upgrade pays for itself this quarter. Three situations justify it.

  • You have hit the three sub-account limit on Starter. A fourth client makes Unlimited necessary.
  • You are absorbing client usage costs. If your agency wallet pays for clients' texts and calls every month, rebilling on Unlimited moves that cost to them.
  • You have customers asking to buy the software itself. When you are ready to charge for access under your own brand, Founder's SaaS Mode automates signup and billing.

If none of those apply, stay where you are. Features on a higher tier that you don't use cost $200 a month.

Common questions

How much does GoHighLevel cost per month?

HighLevel's published plans are Starter at $97 a month, Unlimited at $297 a month and Founder at $497 a month. Texts, calls, phone numbers, emails and most AI features are billed by usage on top of the plan.

Is there a free version of HighLevel?

HighLevel's pricing page doesn't list a free plan. It offers a 14-day free trial on its plans, and a 30-day trial through its live Bootcamp page. Billing starts automatically when a trial ends unless you cancel.

Does HighLevel charge per user or per contact?

No. Every published plan includes unlimited users and unlimited contacts. Your bill grows with messaging and AI usage, not with the size of your team or list.

Is the Founder plan the same as Agency Pro?

Yes. HighLevel's Founder page describes the $497 Founder plan as formerly the Agency Pro plan, and says existing $497 Agency Pro customers are already on it. Some HighLevel pages still use the older names.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. HighLevel's pricing page says plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract, and that you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your dashboard at any time.

Can I use HighLevel for just one business?

Yes. A single business works inside one sub-account. Starter includes three sub-accounts, which is more than a single business needs.
