Why it's confusing

HighLevel has added AI to almost every corner of the platform, and the names overlap. "AI Employee", "AI Agents", "Agent Studio", "AI Studio" and "Ask AI" all sound like the same thing. They aren't.

The simplest way to sort them is by who the AI is working with.

AI that talks to your customers

Conversation AI

A bot that replies to written messages: SMS, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, the website chat widget and live chat. It can answer questions from a knowledge base, collect details, and book, reschedule or cancel appointments. It runs in one of two modes. In Suggestive mode it drafts a reply for a person to send. In Auto-Pilot it replies on its own. Setup guide.

Voice AI

An agent that answers phone calls. It can respond to common questions, take caller details, book appointments, send a text, trigger a workflow and transfer the call to a person. It can also place outbound calls, which needs approval and compliance setup first. Setup guide.

Reviews AI

Drafts replies to customer reviews. You can generate up to four suggestions per review free and are charged a cent when you send an AI-assisted reply.

AI that helps you work

Ask AI

Your own assistant inside HighLevel. Ask it to find contacts, tag a list, summarise a conversation, draft a campaign or explain a setting, and it does it in your account after confirming. Ask AI guide.

Content AI

Writes copy and generates images inside the builders: emails, social posts, funnel text and blog posts.

Builder assistants

Funnel and Website AI, Email AI, Calendar AI, Layout AI and the Workflow AI Builder each help you build one kind of thing from a description. These are free under fair use on every plan.

Creator Studio and AI Studio

Creator Studio generates designs, ads, images and videos from a prompt and is in Labs beta. AI Studio is a separate workspace for building with AI. Both are free for agency users under fair use and metered for sub-account users.

AI that runs work on its own

Managed Agents

Custom AI workers you create by describing a job in plain language. They run on triggers such as a form submission or a tag change, use your knowledge bases, take CRM actions, and can generate images, audio and video. They were previously called Super Agents. Managed Agents guide.

Flow Agents in Agent Studio

The visual, node-by-node way to build an agent on a canvas. More control, more work. Always billed by usage.

Workflow AI Actions

Small AI steps inside a normal workflow: Decision Maker, Intent Detection, Summarize Text and Translate, at one cent per execution. There is also an AI Video Generation action.

What AI in HighLevel won't do

  • It only knows what you give it. A bot with a thin knowledge base guesses or says it doesn't know. Most disappointing results trace back to this.
  • It can be wrong. Prices, availability and policies should come from your knowledge base, and anything that matters should be checked.
  • It costs money per use. Texts and call minutes are charged separately from AI usage, even on unlimited AI plans.
  • It doesn't remove your legal duties. Consent for texts and calls, and disclosure rules for AI callers, still apply. Outbound Voice AI requires approval for this reason.

Common questions

What is the HighLevel AI Employee?

AI Employee is the name for HighLevel's paid AI plans, bought per sub-account. They bundle usage of tools such as Conversation AI, Voice AI, Reviews AI, Content AI and Ask AI. The plans are Starter at $20, Growth at $50 and Unlimited at $97 a month.

Is HighLevel AI included in my plan?

Some of it. Funnel and Website AI, Email AI, Calendar AI, the Knowledge Base and the Workflow AI Builder are free under fair use. Agency users also get Ask AI, AI Studio, Creator Studio and Content AI free under fair use. Conversation AI, Voice AI, Reviews AI, Managed Agents and Workflow AI Actions are paid.

What is the difference between Conversation AI and Voice AI?

Conversation AI replies to written messages on channels such as SMS, web chat, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. Voice AI answers and makes phone calls.

Which HighLevel AI tool should I set up first?

For most service businesses, Conversation AI in Suggestive mode. It drafts replies for a person to approve, which lets you check its accuracy before it talks to customers on its own.
