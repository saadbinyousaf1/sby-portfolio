How to Set Up HighLevel Conversation AI Without Embarrassing Yourself

Set up a HighLevel Conversation AI bot that answers questions and books appointments: knowledge base, goals, Suggestive vs Auto-Pilot, testing and costs.

What Conversation AI does

Conversation AI is a bot that replies to written messages on your behalf. It works across SMS, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, the website chat widget and live chat. Set up well, it answers common questions from your own information, collects a lead's details, and books, reschedules or cancels appointments.

Set up badly, it gives customers wrong answers with total confidence. The difference is almost entirely in two things: what you teach it, and how long you supervise it before letting it loose. This guide is built around both.

Before you start

  • A working HighLevel sub-account with at least one channel connected, such as the chat widget or a registered SMS number
  • A calendar, if you want the bot to book appointments
  • Your real answers to the ten questions customers ask most: prices, hours, service area, what happens next
  • A decision about what the bot must never do, such as quote a final price or give medical or legal advice

If SMS isn't registered yet, do that first. The setup guide covers it.

1.Choose how to build

Go to AI Agents > Conversation AI and click + Create Bot. HighLevel offers three ways in.

MethodSuits
Guided Form SetupBeginners. You fill in fields and pick a bot type such as General Q&A or Appointment Booking
Prompt Based BotPeople comfortable writing detailed instructions. Lets you choose the language model and add actions
Flow Based BuilderBranching, multi-step conversations drawn on a visual canvas

Start with the Guided Form. You can build a more elaborate bot later once you know how yours behaves.

2.Set its identity and voice

Give the bot a name, choose its language and write its first message. Then select or create a Brand Voice, which controls tone and wording. Say plainly that it is an assistant. A bot that pretends to be a person damages trust the moment it slips.

3.Teach it your business

This step decides whether the bot is useful. In the training section, build a knowledge base from:

  • Your website. The crawler can read one exact page, every page under a path, or the whole domain.
  • FAQs you write yourself. Short question-and-answer pairs are the most reliable source.
  • Documents such as price lists and policies.

Write FAQs for anything your website states vaguely or not at all. If you serve customers in more than one language, the documented approach is a separate knowledge base and a separate bot for each language.

4.Give it goals

Goals move the bot beyond answering questions. Typical goals:

  • Collect name, email, phone and the answer to one qualifying question
  • Book an appointment on a named calendar
  • Trigger a workflow when a goal is completed

In a prompt-based bot you can also add actions: appointment booking, workflow triggers, human handover, stop bot, transfer to another bot, automatic follow-up and conversation summaries.

5.Set the guardrails

  • Tell it what to do when it doesn't know: say so and offer a human, never guess.
  • Add a human handover for complaints, refunds and anything urgent.
  • Turn on email notifications for questions the bot couldn't answer. Each one is a gap in your knowledge base.
  • List forbidden topics in its instructions.

6.Test it like a difficult customer

Use the test panel before the bot is live anywhere. HighLevel's own advice is to test lead capture, FAQs, appointment booking, unknown questions and workflow behaviour. Go further:

  • Ask each of your ten common questions in sloppy, misspelled wording
  • Ask something it shouldn't know and check it admits that
  • Ask for a discount it isn't allowed to give
  • Book an appointment, then try to move it
  • Say "I want to speak to a person" and confirm the handover works
  • Reply STOP and confirm it stops

7.Go live in Suggestive mode

A bot has three states: Off, Suggestive and Auto-Pilot. New bots may start as Off.

Switch to Suggestive first. The bot drafts each reply and your team sends, edits or discards it. Run this way for a week or two. Every time you correct a draft, add the right answer to the knowledge base. You are building its accuracy with real conversations and no customer ever sees a mistake.

8.Move to Auto-Pilot, one channel at a time

When you are sending most drafts unedited, turn on Auto-Pilot for a single channel. Web chat is a sensible first choice because visitors expect a bot there. Leave SMS in Suggestive mode a little longer, since a wrong text feels more personal and costs money to send.

Keep reading transcripts weekly. Bots don't get worse on their own, but your prices, hours and offers change, and the knowledge base has to follow.

What it costs

Conversation AI is paid. On pay-per-use you are charged for the language-model tokens each reply uses, at a rate that depends on the model you pick. The AI Employee Growth plan at $50 per sub-account includes 1,000 responses a month, and Unlimited at $97 removes the cap. Text messages the bot sends are charged at normal SMS rates on top. The AI pricing guide has the figures.

Common mistakes

  • Going straight to Auto-Pilot. The single most common cause of a bad first impression.
  • Training on the website alone. Most sites don't state prices, lead times or policies clearly enough.
  • No way to reach a human. Customers forgive a bot that hands over quickly.
  • Running a bot and a workflow that both reply. If a follow-up workflow texts the same contact, the two talk over each other. Pause the workflow when the bot is active.
  • Never reading transcripts. The unanswered-question emails are a free list of improvements.

Common questions

What channels does HighLevel Conversation AI work on?

HighLevel lists SMS, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, the chat widget and live chat.

What is the difference between Suggestive and Auto-Pilot?

In Suggestive mode the bot drafts a reply and a person reviews and sends it. In Auto-Pilot the bot sends replies automatically.

Can Conversation AI book appointments?

Yes. With booking goals or actions configured and a calendar connected, it can book, reschedule and cancel appointments.

How much does Conversation AI cost?

On pay-per-use it is billed by language-model tokens. The AI Employee Growth plan includes 1,000 responses a month for $50 per sub-account, and the Unlimited plan at $97 removes the cap under fair use. SMS charges for the messages it sends are separate.

Does a new bot start replying straight away?

Not necessarily. HighLevel says new bots may be set to Off by default, so check the status and switch it on when you're ready.
