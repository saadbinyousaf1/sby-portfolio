HighLevel AI Pricing: Pay-Per-Use vs AI Employee Plans

What HighLevel's AI really costs: the pay-per-use rates, the $20, $50 and $97 AI Employee plans, what none of them include, and which option fits your volume.

The short answer

HighLevel's AI is charged separately from your main plan, and you have two ways to pay for it: by usage with no monthly fee, or through an AI Employee plan bought for each sub-account.

OptionMonthly, per sub-accountIn one line
Pay-per-use$0Pay only for what the AI actually does
AI Employee Starter$20Content and assistant tools only. Founder-plan agencies only
AI Employee Growth$501,000 chat replies and 100 voice minutes included
AI Employee Unlimited$97Unlimited chat and voice AI, under fair use

From HighLevel's AI Product Pricing article, last modified 10 November 2026 and checked 14 November 2026. HighLevel says prices and models may change.

For light use, pay-per-use is usually the cheapest. The plans buy predictability, and for agencies they are something simple to resell.

What's free anyway

Before paying for anything, note what costs nothing. Funnel and Website AI, Email AI, Calendar AI, Layout AI, the Knowledge Base and the Workflow AI Builder are free on every plan under fair use. If you log in as the agency owner, Ask AI, AI Studio, Creator Studio and Content AI are free for you too.

The tools that always cost money are the ones that work without you: Conversation AI, Voice AI, Reviews AI, Managed Agents and Workflow AI Actions.

HighLevel gives two worked figures of its own. A 10-minute Voice AI call with OpenAI text-to-speech costs $0.60, before model tokens and phone charges. A Conversation AI exchange using 100,000 input tokens and 25,000 output tokens on GPT 5.1 pricing costs $0.375.

Allowances are monthly. Ask AI, AI Studio and Creator Studio usage resets on a rolling five-hour window. "Unlimited" is subject to HighLevel's fair-use policy. Starter is available only to agencies on the $497 Founder plan.

What no plan includes

  • Phone and text charges. Every Voice AI call still pays per-minute phone rates, and every AI-written text still pays SMS rates. This is the cost people most often miss.
  • Workflow AI Actions and the AI Agent workflow action.
  • Agent Studio flow agents, which are pay-per-use on every plan.
  • AI Studio cloud hosting above the free Nano size.

When a Growth allowance runs out, the AI pauses unless you have turned on Extra Usage in AI Suite. It is off by default. Decide which you'd prefer: a bot that stops, or a bill that grows.

Worked examples

These use assumed volumes to show the shape of the decision. Phone and SMS charges are extra in every case.

A quiet local business

Voice AI answers after-hours calls: 40 calls a month at 3 minutes each.

  • 120 minutes × about $0.06 = about $7.20 on pay-per-use, plus model tokens.
  • Growth at $50 covers only 100 of those minutes.

Pay-per-use wins clearly.

A busy clinic

Voice AI takes 1,500 minutes of calls a month and Conversation AI handles web chat all day.

  • 1,500 minutes × about $0.06 = about $90 for voice alone, plus tokens for both tools.
  • Unlimited at $97 covers both, with no surprises.

Unlimited is the safer choice here. Somewhere around 1,600 voice minutes a month is where the $97 plan starts to pay for itself on voice alone, and sooner if you choose a premium voice.

An agency with ten clients

Ten sub-accounts on Unlimited is $970 a month. Enable a plan only for clients who use the AI and are paying you for it. Rebilling AI Employee to clients requires the $497 agency plan.

Which to choose

  • Trying AI for the first time: pay-per-use. Watch your wallet for a month and you'll have real numbers.
  • Steady, moderate use and you want a fixed bill: Growth, with Extra Usage decided deliberately.
  • AI answers most calls and chats: Unlimited.
  • Agency reselling AI as a package: Unlimited per paying client, priced above your cost.

Choose the cheapest text-to-speech voice and language model that sound acceptable. On pay-per-use, that single setting can change your per-minute cost several times over.

Common questions

How much is the HighLevel AI Employee?

HighLevel lists three AI Employee plans, each priced per sub-account per month: Starter at $20 (available only to agencies on the $497 Founder plan), Growth at $50 and Unlimited at $97. You can also use AI with no plan and pay only for usage.

How much does HighLevel Voice AI cost per minute?

On pay-per-use, HighLevel charges a Voice Engine fee of $0.045 per minute plus a text-to-speech fee that starts at $0.015 per minute, so about $0.06 per minute before language-model tokens and phone charges. HighLevel's own example prices a 10-minute call with OpenAI TTS at $0.60.

Does AI Employee Unlimited include phone charges?

No. HighLevel states that phone system charges apply to all calls regardless of plan, and text and call charges are billed separately.

Is the AI Employee plan per account or per sub-account?

Per sub-account. An agency enabling the $97 plan for ten clients pays ten times.

What happens when the Growth plan's allowance runs out?

Usage pauses unless Extra Usage is switched on in AI Suite. It is off by default. With it on, further usage is billed at pay-per-use rates.
