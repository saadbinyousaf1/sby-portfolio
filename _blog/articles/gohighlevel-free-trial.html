The two trials

HighLevel doesn't have a free plan. It has two free trials, and which one you get depends on the page you sign up from.

The Bootcamp is a live session where HighLevel's coaches walk new accounts through first setup. It isn't recorded and is designed to be followed on a laptop or desktop, so only choose it if you'll attend. If you will, 30 days is plainly better than 14.

What still costs money during a trial

"Free" covers the subscription. It doesn't cover usage. If you buy a phone number, send texts, make calls or use paid AI features during the trial, those are charged to your wallet at normal rates.

For a realistic trial this is small. A local number is $1.15 a month and a text is around a cent. A week of careful testing should cost only a few dollars. Just don't import a big list and blast it on day two.

Put the end date in your calendar nowBilling begins automatically when the trial ends. If you decide HighLevel isn't for you, cancel from the dashboard before that date. Set a reminder two days earlier.

Which plan to pick at signup

Starter, for almost everyone. It has every core feature and three sub-accounts. Pick Unlimited only if you already look after more than three client businesses. The pricing guide explains the differences.

Decide what you want to prove

Most trials are wasted. People log in, click around forty menus, feel overwhelmed and let the fortnight run out. They end up paying for something they haven't tested, or cancelling something that would have worked.

Give the trial one job. For most businesses the right test is a single loop:

A stranger fills in a form, gets a reply within a minute, books a time, and shows up in a pipeline.

If that works with your own phone as the "stranger", HighLevel can do the thing it's best at for your business. Everything else can wait.

A seven-day plan

On the 30-day trial you have room for a second and third week: add missed call text back, try Conversation AI in Suggestive mode, and run the system on a handful of real leads.

How to judge the result

  • The loop worked from form to booked appointment
  • You could see where to find things by the end of the week
  • It would replace at least two tools you pay for now
  • The usage cost in your wallet looks affordable at your real volume
  • Someone on your team is willing to own it

Four or five ticks: keep it. Two or fewer: cancel without guilt. HighLevel is the wrong tool for plenty of businesses, and the beginner's guide lists which.

Common trial mistakes

  • Loading a large snapshot on day one. You inherit dozens of automations you don't understand.
  • Skipping email and text setup. Then judging the platform on messages that never arrived.
  • Testing with a contact that already exists. Use a fresh email and number so you see what a new lead sees.
  • Trying every feature. Courses, communities and AI agents will still be there in month two.
  • Forgetting the end date.

If you'd rather skip the guided session and start today, the standard trial is the quicker route.
