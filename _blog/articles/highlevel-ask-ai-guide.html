What Ask AI is

Ask AI is an assistant built into HighLevel. You describe what you want in ordinary language, typed or spoken, and it does the work inside your account. That last part is what separates it from a general chatbot: it can read your contacts and pipelines and act on them.

It helps to be clear about what it is not. HighLevel has three AI products that people mix up:

  • Ask AI helps you, the person logged in, get things done faster.
  • Conversation AI talks to your customers by text and chat.
  • Managed Agents are custom AI workers that run on triggers without you.

Where to open it

  • The Ask AI icon in the top navigation bar.
  • The Ask AI item in the left sidebar, which opens a full-screen workspace.
  • Global Search. If a search finds nothing, you can send the query to Ask AI.

Conversations are saved in a sidebar, and Ask AI remembers preferences you tell it.

What it can't do yet

HighLevel's own help article lists these gaps:

  • Workflows and Campaigns are read-only. It can look at them but not build or edit them.
  • Not connected: Affiliate Manager, Listings, chat widget configuration, the full Sites builder (funnels only), raw call and SMS logs, workflow execution history and IVR call routing.

For building workflows from a description, use the Workflow AI Builder inside Automation instead. It is a separate tool and is free under fair use.

Set up Brand Voice first

Ask AI writes much better content when it knows how your business sounds. Go to Marketing > Brand Boards and set up a Brand Voice with your tone, services and positioning before you ask it for copy. Without one, the writing is generic.

Twelve prompts to copy

Ask AI is more accurate when you name things exactly: the pipeline, the tag, the date range. Swap in your own details.

Finding and fixing data

  • "Show me every opportunity in the Sales pipeline that has been in Quote sent for more than 14 days."
  • "Find contacts created in the last 7 days with no phone number."
  • "Summarise my conversation history with Maria Lopez."
  • "Add the tag past-customer to everyone whose opportunity is marked Won this year."

Content

  • "Write a three-email follow-up sequence for people who requested a quote but didn't book. Friendly, short, one clear link."
  • "Draft five social posts for next week about our spring maintenance offer."
  • "Rewrite this text message so it's under 160 characters and still includes the booking link."

Setup and help

  • "Walk me through A2P registration for this sub-account."
  • "Why isn't my email sending domain verified?"
  • "Create a Conversation AI agent that answers questions about our opening hours and books consultations."

Recurring tasks

  • "Every Monday at 9am, send me a list of opportunities with no activity in 7 days."
  • "Research the three largest competitors for a dental clinic in Leeds and summarise what they offer."

Using it safely

  • Read the confirmation screen. Ask AI asks before it writes, updates or deletes, and shows how many records a bulk action will touch. Check the number matches what you expected.
  • Start narrow. Test a bulk action on a small filtered group before running it across your whole list.
  • Check content before sending. It drafts quickly and sometimes confidently gets a detail wrong. Prices, dates and promises need your eyes.
  • Consent still applies. Being able to bulk-text a list in one sentence doesn't change who agreed to hear from you.
