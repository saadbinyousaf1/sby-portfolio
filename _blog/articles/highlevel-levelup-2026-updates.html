How to read a HighLevel release

LevelUp is HighLevel's annual product event, and the 2026 feature post is very long. Reading it start to finish isn't a good use of an afternoon. What matters for each item is one question: can I use it today?

HighLevel uses four states, and they mean different things.

  • Available. No label on the feature. It should be in your account.
  • Labs. Built, but off until you enable it in Settings > Labs.
  • Private beta. Only selected accounts, usually by sign-up.
  • Founder plan. Tied to the $497 agency plan.

I've sorted the updates most accounts will care about into those groups. Statuses are as HighLevel's post stated them when checked on {{checked}}, and they change quickly.

Usable today

Workflow A/B testing

Split contacts across up to 10 paths in a workflow and let HighLevel route to the winner based on a goal you choose. This is the update I'd use first. Test two versions of your first follow-up text and let the numbers decide.

Loop and Router actions

Loop repeats steps over a list or a number range. Router evaluates every branch and runs all that match, where If/Else runs only one. Both remove workarounds that used to need several workflows.

Built-in Meeting Room

Native video meetings with calendar links, recordings, transcripts and AI summaries, plus breakout rooms. For anyone booking consultations through HighLevel calendars, this removes a separate video-call subscription from the stack.

Ask AI upgrades

Ask AI can now generate and analyse documents, keep project workspaces, be reached by phone call or SMS, and split big requests across parallel subagents. Agencies get a permission matrix for which skills each user can run. See the Ask AI guide.

Managed Agents improvements

Memory of preferences and learned procedures, waits and scheduled follow-ups, inbound webhook triggers, subagents and computer capabilities. See the Managed Agents guide.

AI agent health checks

Monitoring for voice and chat agent quality that proposes prompt fixes for you to review, plus redesigned logs and goal tracking. Voice AI agents can also be cloned to other sub-accounts.

Smaller things worth knowing

  • Email Inbox Preview: see how an email renders in Gmail, Outlook and other clients before sending.
  • Conversations keyword search: search the whole inbox or one thread.
  • Snapshot dependency resolution: snapshots now bundle the assets a workflow needs, such as its email templates.
  • Dashboard AI summaries: a written summary across your widgets, refreshed daily.
  • Conversation AI date awareness: bots handle "today" and "tomorrow" correctly and avoid double-booking.

Private beta

  • Opportunities overhaul: deals without a contact, a new sidebar workspace, deal-level conversation feeds, appointments and custom associations.
  • Ticketing: support tickets with ownership, priority and SLA tracking.
  • Workflows API and MCP: create and monitor workflows by code, or let an AI assistant read and build them.
  • RCS messaging: branded rich messaging with sender registration.
  • HighLevel Industries: purpose-built versions for field services, restaurants and business coaching.

Don't promise any of these to a client yet.

Founder plan

  • Workflow Maps: a live map of every workflow and what it connects to. Full guide.
  • Custom navigation: agencies can reshape the menu, categories and layouts and reuse them as templates across sub-accounts.
  • Industries: the industry products above are Founder offerings.

This is a clear direction. HighLevel is putting its newest agency tools on the $497 tier. The pricing guide covers when that upgrade pays for itself.

What I'd do this week

  1. Add an A/B test to the first message in your main lead workflow.
  2. Open Labs and read the list. Enable Creator Studio in your own account and try it.
  3. If you take video consultations, test Meeting Room on one calendar.
  4. If you run an agency on the Founder plan, open Workflow Maps on your messiest client account and run Cleanup.
  5. Ignore the private-beta list until something on it solves a problem you have today.
